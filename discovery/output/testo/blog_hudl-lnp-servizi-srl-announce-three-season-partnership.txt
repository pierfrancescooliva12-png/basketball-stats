Hudl & LNP Servizi SRL Announce Three-Season Partnership
15 AUG 2025 1 MIN READ

Lincoln, Nebraska, 15 August 2025 - LNP Servizi, the organisation responsible for the administration of the Italian Serie A2 and Serie B basketball leagues, and Hudl, a global leader in sports performance analysis and scouting, have announced a renewal of their collaboration for scouting rights to its competitions.

The three-season agreement, from 2025-26 to 2027-28, includes both the second division, the Italian Serie A2, and the third division, Serie B. Hudl will gain access to all related video and data, which will be made available for professional purposes via Instat—the comprehensive analysis and scouting solution.

“Our commitment is to empower coaches, players and teams with cutting-edge analysis tools,” said Hudl’s Jose Gallart. “The collaboration with LNP Servizi is exciting, as it enables us to continue to further our expertise in the Italian basketball market."

About Hudl

Hudl empowers more than 325,000 teams globally to reach their potential, working with coaches, athletes and administrators at every level of sport. We equip teams with tools and insights to elevate performance, streamline operations, drive recruitment and deepen fan engagement. Our smart cameras capture every second of practices and games, powering the industry's ecosystem of film review, recruiting, scouting and more. Our trusted software layers data and analytics onto video to help athletes and teams reach their potential.
